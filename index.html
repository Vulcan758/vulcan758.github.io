01About

I'm a Research Assistant in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at BRAC University, working on SLAM back-end optimization. Before that I completed a B.Sc. in Applied Physics and Electronics there (2026), with a thesis on fast sampling-based motion planning. Most of what I know I learned building BRACU Mongol-Tori, a Mars rover prototype for the University Rover Challenge — I wrote its autonomous navigation stack from scratch, then led the 30+ person team.

I'm happiest owning a system end to end: reading a planning paper, implementing it, and seeing it run on hardware I helped design. Right now I'm working on SLAM for GNSS-denied navigation, focusing on the back end: pose graph optimization.

I'm looking for my first industry role in robotics autonomy — field and delivery robots, space robotics, defense autonomy, or assistive robotics. Remote or Toronto/GTA, open to relocation.
